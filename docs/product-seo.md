# Product SEO Guide

Product SEO is the process of optimizing product pages so they can rank higher in search engines and attract customers who are ready to buy.

## Why Product SEO Matters

Optimized product pages help businesses:

- Increase organic traffic
- Improve product visibility
- Generate qualified leads
- Increase online sales
- Improve user experience

## Product SEO Checklist

- Write a unique product title
- Add a compelling meta description
- Use descriptive URLs
- Optimize product images
- Add Product Schema
- Include FAQs where relevant
- Improve page loading speed

## Common Mistakes

- Duplicate product descriptions
- Missing image alt text
- Poor internal linking
- Thin content
- Slow loading pages
